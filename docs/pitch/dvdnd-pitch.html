05How it works

From raise to dividend, on-chain.

01 · LIST

Prove it's yours

Register lookup fills the profile. DNS proof of domain. Profit-share agreement hashed on-chain.

02 · RAISE

One price

Min, max, deadline. Reg S, Rule 506(c) or both. Flat listing fee.

03 · PROVE

Eligible, privately

Didit identity check, then a Groth16 proof of non-US residence verified by Soroban.

04 · CLOSE

Paid or refunded

USDC in escrow goes to the company or back to investors. No other path.

05 · EARN

Paid to the patient

Tokens earn in the vault. Only full-period lockers share the dividend.

06 · TRADE

Among the eligible

After lock-up, Stellar's order book, restricted to authorized wallets.

Money reaches the company once, at the raise, and flows back to holders as dividends. No bonding curve, no sniper advantage, no buyback.

07Compliance by design

No cut. No custody. No gatekeeper.

RuleHow dvdnd enforces it
Securities Act §4(b)A flat listing fee per raise. Never a share of capital raised: software, not a broker.
Customer fundsEscrowed USDC can only go to the company on success or back to investors. There's no withdraw button.
Regulation SNon-US residence proven with a zero-knowledge proof, checked by the contract.
Rule 506(c)Verified accredited investors, US included: an attestation, or $200k minimum ($1M for entities).
Rule 144 · §12(g)One-year Reg D lock-up and a holder cap under 2,000, both enforced on-chain.
Who may holdStellar's AUTH_REQUIRED flags refuse transfers to unverified wallets, on every venue.
SEC Release 33-11412, with the CFTC (17 March 2026): a tokenized security is still a security, so on-chain shares use the same proven exemptions as any private raise. Draft strategy, not legal advice.

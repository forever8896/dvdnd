Four neighbourhood bakeries, profitable since 2014

Harbor & Hearth runs four sourdough bakeries across Porto and Matosinhos. Each shop was profitable within 14 months of opening. The raise funds a central production kitchen that cuts ingredient and labour cost per loaf by roughly a fifth, and a fifth shop in Gaia planned for 2027.

The company commits 50% of audited yearly net profit to token holders for as long as the business operates. Dividends are paid quarterly in USDC.
